# Calculation model

```
Base gross price      = fixed price (incl. VAT)  or  (materials + labour + tool depr. + overhead) × (1 + markup) × (1 + VAT)
+ Extra services      = service price × qty × (1 + VAT)
= Gross price (customer pays)
− VAT                 B2C: market VAT (destination principle: domestic, EU OSS, import VAT)
                      B2B cross-border: 0 (reverse charge / export); small-business exemption: 0 (domestic only)
− Payment fee         gross × fee % + fixed fee
− Sales commission    gross × commission %
− Shipping            cost per shipment + cost per kg × weight
− Customs             (B2C, different customs territories) duty % × (net price + shipping) + clearance fee
− Returns             return rate × (2 × shipping + handling cost)
− Production cost     materials × (1 + waste %) + labour h × HQ labour rate + service labour
                      + tools / useful life / annual volume + legal-form annual fixed costs / annual volume
= Profit before tax
− Social contributions (% of profit, sole proprietors)   − Profit / income tax   = Net profit of the company
− Distribution (dividend) tax                                                     = NET TO OWNER

Startup cash   = formation costs + max(min. share capital, tools + startup material stock)
Break-even     = gross price at which profit before tax = 0
```

Simplifications: flat effective tax rates (progressive brackets such as Croatia's 10 %/18 % or Montenegro's 9–15 % are entered as the rate that applies to you), material prices fall back to the latest earlier year with a warning.
